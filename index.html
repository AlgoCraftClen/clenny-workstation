Accounting guardrails

Asset cost follows the asset

A box owner pays the supplier cost and any charge directly attributable to that specific box.

Shared work follows venture capital

Funding is not responsibility

Who paid determines reimbursement. The ownership basis determines who ultimately bears the cost.
